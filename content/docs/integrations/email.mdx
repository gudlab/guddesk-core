The email integration lets you manage support emails alongside chat conversations in your GudDesk shared inbox.

## How It Works

1. Forward incoming emails to your GudDesk support address
2. Emails appear as conversations in your shared inbox
3. Reply from GudDesk. Your response is sent as a regular email
4. Later emails in the same thread are added to the same conversation

## Setting Up Email Forwarding

### 1. Find Your Support Address

Each workspace has its own support address in this form:

```
support+<your-app-id>@mail.guddesk.com
```

Find yours in **Workspace Settings > Email Channel**, under **Support Address**.

### 2. Configure Forwarding

Set up email forwarding from your support email to your GudDesk support address:

**Gmail:**
1. Go to Settings > Forwarding and POP/IMAP
2. Add your GudDesk support address as a forwarding address
3. Confirm the forwarding request

**Custom domain:**
Set up a mail forwarding rule with your email provider to forward `support@yourdomain.com` to your GudDesk support address.

### 3. Outbound Settings

Under **Outbound Settings** you can set the from name, the from address and an email signature. GudDesk sends replies through Resend. A custom from address only works if its domain is verified in the Resend account that sends your mail, which on a self-hosted install is your own account.

## Email Threading

Replies include `In-Reply-To` and `References` headers, so customers see a normal email thread in their mail app.

## Attachments

Attachments are not imported yet. The text of each email arrives in the conversation, but attached files don't.

## Auto-Reopen

With **Auto-reopen on reply** turned on (the default), a closed conversation reopens when the customer sends a new email reply. GudDesk does not send automatic acknowledgment emails.

## Self-Hosted Email

For self-hosted instances, configure these environment variables:

```env
RESEND_API_KEY="your-resend-api-key"
```

GudDesk uses [Resend](https://resend.com) for email delivery. You can use the free tier (100 emails/day) for small teams or upgrade for higher volume.
