Update, November 2026: GudBot is available. It is optional and off by default. When you turn it on, it answers from your knowledge base with citations and hands off to a human when it isn't sure. You can also connect your own agent through the API.

Customer support tools have a problem. They're either expensive, locked-in, or both. When we needed a modern messaging platform for our own projects, we kept running into the same wall: pay $50+ per seat per month for features that should be table stakes, or duct-tape together a dozen open-source tools that don't talk to each other.

So we built GudDesk.

## What is GudDesk?

GudDesk is a complete customer messaging platform - live chat, shared inbox, knowledge base, an AI agent API, and SLA policies - all bundled into a single open-source project licensed under AGPL-3.0.

Think of it as the Intercom experience without the Intercom price tag.

## Why We Built It

Three things frustrated us about the current landscape:

1. **Per-seat pricing is broken.** Your entire team should be able to jump into a conversation without your CFO having a panic attack.
2. **GudBot is optional.** It stays off by default. When you turn it on, it answers from your knowledge base with citations and hands off to a human when it isn't sure. You can also connect your own agent through the API.
3. **You should own your data.** Full stop. Self-host it, export it, move it wherever you want.

GudDesk addresses all three.

## What's Included

Out of the box, you get:

- **Live Chat Widget** - A lightweight, embeddable widget you can drop into any site with two lines of code.
- **Shared Inbox** - Chat and bot conversations in one view for your whole team. You can send replies by email. Incoming customer email on Cloud is still being set up.
- **Knowledge Base**: write help articles and organise them into collections.
- **GudBot**: optional, off by default. When enabled, it answers from your knowledge base with citations and hands off when it isn't sure. You can also connect your own agent through the API.
- **Automation rules**: a rule builder for conversation events. Rules are saved but don't run yet.
- **Plugin API** - Build custom integrations on the REST API and webhooks.

## Free for Small Teams

GudDesk is free to use for teams of 2 members, 500 conversations/month. No credit card required, and there is no time-limited trial. If you're a startup or a small team, you can run your entire support operation on GudDesk without paying a cent.

For larger teams, Pro lifts the member and conversation limits and removes GudDesk branding.

## Self-Hostable

Every line of GudDesk is open source. Clone the gudlab/guddesk-core repo, install it with pnpm, point it at your own PostgreSQL database and run it on Node.js 20.19 or later. The README lists each step. Your data never leaves your servers.

## What's Next

We're just getting started. Here's what's on the roadmap:

- **Slack and Teams integrations** - Reply to customers from where your team already works.
- **Custom agent builder** - A visual editor for creating AI agent workflows.
- **Mobile app** - Native iOS and Android apps for on-the-go support.
- **Multilingual knowledge base** - Auto-translate articles for global teams.
